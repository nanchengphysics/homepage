Research

Hyperbolic lattices and generalized band theory

Generalizes Bloch theorem to hyperbolic lattices. This new Bloch theorem involves high-dimensional irreducible representations applies systems with nonabelian translation groups.
Studies band theory, boundary modes, degeneracy, and mode counting in non-Euclidean crystals using this new Bloch theorem.

Non-Hermitian spectral moments

Establish a universal spectral moment theorem giving universal constraints for the spectra of non-Hermitian systems.
Apply the universal spectral moment theorem to the bulk dynamics of non-Hermitian systems and find a new bulk dynamical phase.

Frustrated self-assembly and hyperbolic crystals

Study how geometric frustration can guide self-limited assembly by developing an analytic theory of frustrated self-assembly of icosahedral nanoparticles based on the hyperbolic crystal {3,5,3}.

Backscattering-free edge states in auxetic media

Show spin and momentum of the Rayleigh waves in passive linear elastic media with vanishing bulk modulus are locked, yielding one-way edge states below all bulk bands.
Show such one-way edge waves is protected by a topological winding number and thus back-scattering free.
The conclusion above is verified numerically and experimentally.

Gapped phonon spectrum of hyperbolic lattices at zero frequency

Argue from the representation theoretical point of view that the phonon spectrum of over-constraint infinite periodic hyperbolic lattices can be gapped at zero frequency.
Numerically show that the low frequency phonon density of states of infinite periodic {7,3} and {8,8} hyperbolic lattices are gapped at zero frequency using the loop counting method developed in the Non-Hermitian spectral moments, and prove such gap is experimentally measurable on a finite patch of hyperbolic lattices.

Design developable origami by symmetry

Develop a complete layer-group classification of symmetry-protected linear folding modes for triangulated origami.
Extract a symmetry-and-topology-based design principal on nonlinear curvature-free folding from the classification.

Physics, Geometry, and Mechanics

The Geometric Interpretation of Berry Phase

This note introduces Berry phase from the viewpoint of tangent spaces, differential forms, vector bundles, covariant derivatives, curvature, and holonomy, with an introduction to Chern classes and Chern numbers. Bulk-edge correspondence is not included here.

PDF · 14 pages · Download

Minimal Coupling and the Maxwell-Dirac Equations

This note tries to explain the Maxwell-Dirac equations in the language of principal bundles. From this point of view, the minimality of minimal coupling has a natural explaination.

PDF · 3 pages · Download

Noether's Theorem

This note defines the action of groups on classical fields, states and proves Noether's theorem for classical fields using the actions principle.

PDF · 3 pages · Download

Linear Elasticity

This note first shows how elastic energy density under small deformation is determined by locality and symmetry.
This note then derives the Navier–Cauchy equation from Lagrangian mechanics, intorduces strain and stress from the Navier–Cauchy equation and explains their physical meaning.
Finally this note introduces bulk and surface waves as solutions to the Navier–Cauchy equation.
This note was prepared for a summer course on linear elasticity.

PDF · 14 pages · Download

Symmetries in Quantum Mechanics

Hilbert-space quantum mechanics, unitary and antiunitary symmetries, time reversal, and linear and projective representations, including spin, SO(3), and SU(2).
